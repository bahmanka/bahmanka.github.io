01ABOUT

Art begins where certainty ends.

I am Bahman Kiani Azad, an artist and art researcher born in 1988. My practice is rooted in expressionism, shaped by a romantic sensibility and a narrative approach. I am drawn to the complexity and contradictions of human experience: solitude and anxiety, beauty and suffering, vulnerability and resilience.

Rather than offering definitive answers, I use images and visual narratives to give form to inner experiences and unresolved questions. Alongside painting and research, I explore digital media, interactive experiences and independent creative projects. Across these different fields, I am interested in how stories, images and systems shape the way we understand ourselves and the world.
